---
name: Midweek odds refresh
description: Preserve pregame Thursday odds when refreshing current-week lines on Sunday.
---

The league normally loads the week's odds on Wednesday, including the Thursday
night game. Staff may rerun odds on Sunday morning when favorites are close.
Keep the Wednesday odds for games that have already started; refresh only games
that have not kicked off. A completed game may be missing from Sunday's odds
feed altogether.

**Why:** The user wants a Sunday comparison without Thursday's postgame or
in-play prices changing the biggest-favorite selection or fees.

**How to apply:** Do not clear stored odds for the whole week before a refresh.
Preserve both matchup odds and team-level odds for started or omitted games.
Do not run a live odds refresh merely to test this behavior; the user will
choose when to run it.
