---
name: Underdog half-loss credits
description: Winning picks on underdogs of at least five points earn record-only credits.
---

The user specified unlimited half-loss credits for correctly picking an underdog
of five points or more. Each qualifying winning pick still counts as a normal
win and earns half a loss back toward the player's record. Pot contributions
are not affected.

**Why:** The user distinguishes a record credit from the money owed for losses.

**How to apply:** Keep actual losses and their fees separate from adjusted
record losses. Include exactly five-point underdogs in the threshold. Do not
refund fees or remove the qualifying win when applying a credit.

Bank unused credits for future losses. Adjusted losses must not go below zero,
and unused credits must not be discarded.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose to bank credits when there are no losses
left to offset.

**How to apply:** Keep total credits earned separate from credits used. Later
losses automatically consume banked credits, without changing any loss fees.
